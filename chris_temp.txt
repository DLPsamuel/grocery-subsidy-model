Worth asking while there. 4. Tier 3 — Must be estimated, with the reasoning documented θ — subsidy pass-through The least-identified number in the model. It should be presented that way rather than quietly assigned, because every dollar of ΔCS in our results scales directly with it. Two problems with borrowing it from the existing pass-through literature: Different object. Those studies report elasticities from log-log regressions (a 1% wholesale price change producing an x% retail price change) on branded packaged goods at a single supermarket chain. Our θ is a fraction of subsidy dollars reaching shoppers, via Δp_j = θ·s/Q_j. Converting between the two is not a matter of copying a number across. Different mechanism. Those papers measure a per-unit wholesale cost shock. Our tax break and rent subsidy are fixed-cost reductions. Standard theory says a fixed-cost cut should not move a profit-maximiser's prices at all, since prices are set off marginal cost — which would imply θ = 0 for our instruments. The reasons real-world pass-through is not zero (the store is not a textbook profit-maximiser, subsidy conditions may attach strings, or the subsidy prevents exit and keeps a competitor in the market) belong in the write-up as an argument, not buried inside a parameter choice. Recommended handling: treat θ as unidentified. Run the full 0.40 / 0.65 / 0.85 range and report whether the Goal 2 store ranking flips across it. If the ranking holds, θ does not drive our conclusion and we can say so plainly. If it flips, that is itself the finding. β_p_i — final values The literature reports willingness to pay in distance, not logit coefficients in dollars per basket. Once Taylor &amp; Villas-Boas is in hand, anchor β_d from Tier 1 and back out β_p using the model's own identity, WTP = β_d / β_p , rather than assuming both independently. That keeps the ratio consistent with published behaviour even where levels need rescaling. β_d_i — income gradient Published gradients are by SNAP status or broad income terciles, not our &lt;$25k / $25–50k / &gt;$50k bands. Map published groups onto our three bands and scale by the CD2 car-free share (71.9%). Document the mapping in a comment block in params.py so the choice stays auditable. 5.
